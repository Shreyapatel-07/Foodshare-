01 — ABOUT US

Why FoodShare?

Food waste is a major problem in communities around the world. Restaurants, hotels, events and households often have extra food that is still safe to eat. At the same time, many people need access to regular meals.

FoodShare is a simple web-based platform designed to connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers. Donors can share information about available surplus food, while volunteers can help collect and deliver it to people who need it.

Our goal is to reduce unnecessary food waste and encourage responsible consumption. The project supports SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production while also contributing to a healthier and more sustainable community.

Our Mission

"Reduce food waste by connecting surplus food with people who need it."
